Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

Lot Invaders Mega is an arcade game for iPhone and iPad. The game does not require an account and does not ask you to submit personal profile information.

Data Stored On Your Device

The game stores progress, settings, garage purchases, currency balance, rewarded-ad cooldown state, and purchase grant history locally on your device using Apple storage such as UserDefaults and Keychain.

In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive purchase status from StoreKit so the game can grant purchased cash packs or one-time packs. We do not receive your payment card details.

Advertising And Consent

The game uses Google AdMob rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. There are no forced interstitial ads. Before ads are requested, Google User Messaging Platform may ask for consent where required, and iOS may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. Google may process identifiers, approximate location, diagnostics, and ad interaction data according to your consent choices and Google policies.

Tracking

If you allow tracking through the iOS prompt and consent where applicable, advertising identifiers may be used for ad personalization and measurement. If you deny permission or do not consent, ads may still be shown when available, but they should not use the IDFA for tracking.

Children

Lot Invaders Mega is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the support page: support.html.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the game, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
